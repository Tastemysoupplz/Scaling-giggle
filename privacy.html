Privacy Policy

Goblin Stew · Effective date: November 24, 2026

The short version: our games are single-player and play offline. We have no accounts and no servers, and we do not sell or share your personal information. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the game can unlock achievements and sync cloud saves through your Google account. That data is held by Google, not by us.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Goblin Stew ("we", "us", "our") develops and publishes mobile games. This policy applies to every game we publish on Google Play and any other store, including:

  • Cairnhollow
  • Junkhaven
  • Kindled Ages
  • Below the Glow
  • Deadwake
  • Any other game published under the Goblin Stew name

It also covers this website. If a particular game works differently, that game's store page or in-game notice will say so.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. Our games do not ask you to create an account with us or give us your email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other personal data. We do not run our own servers that receive data from the games, and nothing you do in the games is sent to us.

Our games do not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting tools that send information about you to us or to third parties.

3. Google Play Games sign-in, achievements, and cloud saves

The games save your progress, settings, and similar gameplay data on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see this data. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear the game's storage in your device settings.

Our games may offer sign-in with Google Play Games Services. Signing in is optional, and the games can be played without it. If you sign in, the game can:

  • Read your Play Games profile information that Google shares with the game, such as your username (gamertag), avatar, and a player ID
  • Record achievements (trophies) and similar progress to your Play Games profile
  • Back up your progress with cloud save, so you can continue on another Android device

We use this information only to run these features inside the game. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with other data to track you. Your achievements and cloud saves are stored by Google, linked to your Google Play Games profile, under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google may also collect analytics and diagnostics through Play Games Services as described in its own policy. We do not keep a copy of your profile or saves on any system of our own.

You can manage or delete your Play Games data, including cloud saves and achievements, through your Play Games profile and your Google Account. You can stop using these features at any time by signing out of Google Play Games or not signing in. Progress saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

4. Third-party services

Our games are distributed through Google Play and use Google Play Games Services for optional sign-in, achievements, and cloud saves. When you download or buy a game, or use Google Play Games features like sign-in, achievements, or cloud save, Google collects and processes information under its own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). That includes your purchase and payment details, which we never receive, and your achievements and cloud save data, which are stored by Google. Google Play may also provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash totals, which do not identify you.

If a future game uses another third-party service (for example ads or analytics), we will update this policy and the game's store listing before it is released, and will say what is collected and why.

5. Children's privacy

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because we do not collect personal data on any system of our own, there is nothing for us to delete or hand over. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

6. Data security

Your game data is stored on your device and, if you use cloud save, in your Google account. Its security depends on your device's protections such as a screen lock and system updates, and on Google's protections for your account. We do not store your data on any system of our own.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. Since we do not hold personal data about you on any system of our own, there is nothing for us to provide or delete (your Play Games profile, achievements, and cloud saves are held by Google and you can manage them with Google), but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question and we will respond.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also note it on the relevant game's store page.
